2. ADMIN ACCESS AND LOGIN

[Demo Image: Admin login page showing IC number and password fields with role selection]

Understanding Admin Access

Administrator access provides full system control and the ability to manage all aspects of the institution. This access must be protected and used responsibly. Only authorized individuals should have administrator accounts.

How Admin Accounts Are Created

Administrator accounts are typically created during initial system setup by a technical administrator or system installer. Additional administrator accounts can be created by existing administrators through the user management interface.

Admin Account Requirements

To become an administrator, you must:
- Have authorization from the institution leadership
- Complete administrator training
- Understand system responsibilities
- Agree to security and privacy policies
- Receive account credentials from a system administrator

Accessing the Login Page

Step 1: Open Your Web Browser

1. Launch your preferred web browser on your computer
2. Supported browsers include Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, or Safari
3. Ensure your browser is up to date for best performance

Step 2: Navigate to the System

1. Enter the system web address in your browser's address bar
2. The address is provided by your technical administrator or system installer
3. Press Enter to load the login page
4. Wait for the page to fully load

Step 3: Verify You Are on the Login Page

You should see:
- The MyMasjidApp logo or system name
- A login form with IC Number and Password fields
- Option to select login type (Regular Login, Student Login, or Staff Check-in)
- Links for registration and password recovery
- Current date and time display

If you do not see these elements, verify you have the correct web address. Contact your technical administrator if you cannot access the login page.

Logging In as Administrator

Step 1: Select Login Type

1. On the login page, locate the login type tabs or buttons
2. Click on the Regular Login tab or button
3. This is the default login type for administrators
4. The login form will display fields for IC Number and Password

Step 2: Enter Your IC Number

1. Locate the IC Number field in the login form
2. This field shows "at masjid.com" after it, indicating it is your unique identifier
3. Enter your twelve-digit Malaysian identity card number
4. You can enter it with or without hyphens (e.g., 051003060229 or 051003-06-0229)
5. The system will automatically format the number as you type
6. Verify the number is correct before proceeding

Important: Your IC number must match exactly the IC number registered in your administrator account. Contact your system administrator if you are unsure of your registered IC number.

Step 3: Enter Your Password

1. Locate the Password field below the IC Number field
2. Click in the password field to activate it
3. Type your administrator password
4. Passwords are case-sensitive, so ensure caps lock is not enabled
5. The password is hidden by default (shown as dots or asterisks)
6. Click the eye icon if you need to see what you typed for verification

Important: Administrator passwords should be strong and secure. Use a combination of letters, numbers, and special characters. Never share your password with anyone.

Step 4: Select Your Role

1. If you have multiple roles in the system, a role selection dropdown will appear
2. Click on the role dropdown to see available roles
3. Select Administrator or Admin from the list
4. Other roles might include Teacher, PIC, or IB if you have those permissions
5. For administrative work, always select the Administrator role

If you do not see a role dropdown, the system will automatically use your primary role. Contact your system administrator if you cannot select the administrator role.

Step 5: Click the Login Button

1. Review your IC number and password one final time
2. Verify the correct role is selected
3. Click the Login button
4. Wait for the system to process your login
5. Do not click the button multiple times

Step 6: Wait for Authentication

1. The system will verify your credentials
2. This process may take a few seconds
3. A loading indicator may appear
4. Do not navigate away during this process
5. The system checks your IC number, password, and role permissions

Step 7: Successful Login

If login is successful:
1. You will be redirected to the administrator dashboard
2. Your name and role will appear at the top right of the screen
3. The navigation menu will show all administrator options
4. The dashboard will display administrative statistics and information
5. You can now access all administrative features

Login Troubleshooting

If Login Fails with Incorrect Credentials Message:

1. Verify your IC number is entered correctly
2. Check that your password is correct and case-sensitive
3. Ensure caps lock is not enabled
4. Try using the Show Password option to verify your entry
5. Clear the password field and re-enter it carefully
6. Contact your system administrator to verify your account credentials

If Login Fails with Account Not Found Message:

1. Verify you are using the correct IC number
2. Check that your account exists in the system
3. Contact your system administrator to confirm your account status
4. Verify you have an administrator account (not just a teacher or student account)
5. Ask your system administrator to check if your account was created correctly

If Login Fails with Account Pending Message:

1. Administrator accounts should not be in pending status
2. Contact your system administrator immediately
3. Your account may need to be activated
4. Do not attempt to log in repeatedly with a pending account
5. Wait for administrator approval before trying again

If Login Fails with Account Inactive Message:

1. Your administrator account may have been deactivated
2. Contact your system administrator immediately
3. Do not attempt to log in repeatedly
4. Your account may need to be reactivated by another administrator
5. Verify why your account was deactivated

If Login Fails with Unauthorized Access Message:

1. Verify you selected the Administrator role
2. Check that your account has administrator permissions
3. Contact your system administrator to verify your role assignment
4. Your account may have the wrong role assigned
5. Ask your system administrator to check your permissions

If Your Session Expires:

1. Administrator sessions typically last 24 hours
2. If your session expires, you will be logged out automatically
3. Simply log in again using your credentials
4. Your work will be saved if you saved it before the session expired
5. Unsaved work may be lost, so save frequently

Password Security for Administrators

Creating Strong Passwords:

1. Use at least eight characters (longer is better)
2. Include uppercase letters (A-Z)
3. Include lowercase letters (a-z)
4. Include numbers (0-9)
5. Include special characters (!@#$%^&*)
6. Avoid using personal information (names, birthdays, IC numbers)
7. Avoid common words or patterns
8. Use a unique password for this system

Changing Your Password:

1. Log into the system
2. Click on your name in the top right corner
3. Select Account Settings or Personal Settings
4. Find the Change Password section
5. Enter your current password
6. Enter your new password
7. Confirm your new password
8. Click Save or Update Password
9. Log out and log back in to verify the new password works

Password Best Practices:

1. Never share your administrator password with anyone
2. Never write your password down where others can see it
3. Change your password regularly (every 90 days recommended)
4. Use different passwords for different systems
5. Do not use the same password for personal and work accounts
6. If you suspect your password is compromised, change it immediately
7. Log out when finished using the system
8. Never leave your computer unattended while logged in

Password Recovery for Administrators

If You Forget Your Password:

1. On the login page, click the Forgot Password link
2. Enter your IC number
3. Select password recovery method (Email or SMS)
4. Follow the recovery instructions sent to your email or phone
5. Create a new password following security guidelines
6. Log in with your new password
7. Contact your system administrator if recovery methods do not work

Important: If you cannot recover your password and you are the only administrator, you may need technical support to reset your account. Contact your system installer or technical support team.

Account Security Recommendations

Protecting Your Administrator Account:

1. Always log out when finished, especially on shared computers
2. Never share your login credentials with anyone
3. Use a secure, private computer for administrative work when possible
4. Avoid logging in on public or unsecured networks
5. Keep your browser and operating system updated
6. Use antivirus software on your computer
7. Be cautious of phishing attempts (fake login pages)
8. Verify you are on the correct system URL before logging in
9. Report any suspicious activity immediately
10. Review your account activity regularly if this feature is available

Multiple Administrator Accounts

If your institution has multiple administrators:

1. Each administrator should have their own account
2. Never share administrator accounts
3. Each administrator should use their own IC number and password
4. Account activity is tracked per administrator
5. Sharing accounts makes it impossible to track who made changes
6. If you need additional administrator accounts, create them through user management

First-Time Admin Login

If this is your first time logging in as administrator:

1. Log in using the credentials provided by your system installer
2. You will be taken to the administrator dashboard
3. Review the dashboard to familiarize yourself with the interface
4. Check your profile information to ensure it is correct
5. Change your password from the default password if one was provided
6. Review system settings and configuration
7. Read any welcome messages or announcements
8. Explore the navigation menu to understand available features

Completing Your Admin Profile

After first login, you should:

1. Click on your name in the top right corner
2. Select Profile or Account Settings
3. Review all your personal information
4. Update contact information if needed
5. Verify your email address is correct
6. Verify your phone number is correct
7. Ensure your address is current
8. Save any changes you make

Important: Keep your profile information current. This information is used for system notifications and account recovery. If your contact information changes, update it immediately.

Understanding Admin Permissions

As an administrator, you have access to:

1. All user accounts (view, create, edit, delete)
2. All student records (view, create, edit, assign to classes)
3. All teacher records (view, create, edit, assign to classes)
4. All classes (create, edit, configure, manage)
5. All attendance records (view, edit, correct)
6. All fee records (view, generate, manage, override)
7. All exam records (create, edit, manage)
8. All result records (view, edit, correct)
9. All payment approvals (view, approve, reject, override)
10. System settings and configuration
11. Academic year management
12. Reports and analytics
13. Announcements and communications
14. Audit logs and activity tracking

With these permissions comes responsibility. Always consider the impact of your actions on the system and users. Use your permissions wisely and maintain data accuracy.

What Happens After Login

After successfully logging in as administrator:

1. You are redirected to the administrator dashboard
2. Your administrator role is active for this session
3. All administrator menu items are visible in the navigation
4. Dashboard displays administrative statistics and information
5. You can access all system features
6. Your session remains active for 24 hours or until you log out
7. Your activity is logged for audit purposes

The administrator dashboard is your starting point for all administrative tasks. Familiarize yourself with the dashboard layout and navigation before proceeding to manage the system.
