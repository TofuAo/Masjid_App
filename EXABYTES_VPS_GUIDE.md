# Exabytes VPS - Correct Plan Selection Guide

## ⚠️ CRITICAL: You Need VPS, NOT Shared Hosting

The **EBiz 12** plans you're looking at are **Shared Hosting** - they will NOT work for Docker.

---

## ✅ What to Look For on Exabytes Website

### ❌ DON'T Choose These (Shared Hosting):
- ❌ **EBiz 12** series (Mini, Plus, Pro, Max)
- ❌ **Web Hosting** plans
- ❌ **WordPress Hosting** plans
- ❌ Plans with **PLESK** or **cPanel** control panel
- ❌ Plans with **Entry Process (EP)** limits

### ✅ DO Choose These (VPS):
- ✅ **VPS Hosting** or **Cloud VPS**
- ✅ **KVM VPS** or **OpenVZ VPS**
- ✅ Plans with **Full Root Access**
- ✅ Plans with **SSH Access**
- ✅ Plans with **Ubuntu/Debian/CentOS** OS options
- ✅ Plans that mention **Docker support** or allow custom installations

---

## 🎯 Recommended Exabytes VPS Plans

### For Early Stage (Your Current Needs):

**VPS Starter / Basic:**
- **RAM:** 1GB (minimum) / 2GB (recommended)
- **CPU:** 1-2 cores
- **Storage:** 20-40GB SSD
- **Bandwidth:** 1-2TB/month
- **OS:** Ubuntu 22.04 LTS
- **Price:** RM 30-60/month
- **Location:** Cyberjaya or Penang

**Features You Need:**
- ✅ Full root/SSH access
- ✅ Can install Docker & Docker Compose
- ✅ Custom port access (80, 443, 5000, 3306)
- ✅ Persistent storage for database and uploads
