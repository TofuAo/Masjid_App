# IB Function – Fees Management Authority

This document defines the authority of the IB (Institution Board) function over fees management in MyMasjidApp.

---

## Official policy (clear & professional)

**IB Function – Fees Management**

Fees management is a core responsibility of the IB function and is treated as the highest priority. **Any action by IB is always first priority.** No external approval is required for this process. The IB role holds full authority to approve all fee-related matters independently. Payment processing is a critical activity under the IB function.

---

## Alternative phrasings

### Version 2 – Formal / policy style

The IB function has full ownership and priority over fees management. No approval from any other department or individual is required. The IB function is the sole authority for approving all fee-related actions. Payment processing is a crucial activity managed entirely by the IB function.

### Version 3 – Short & direct

Fees management is a top-priority function of IB. The IB function has complete authority to approve all related activities without requiring approval from any other party. Payment processing is a critical responsibility of IB.

### Version 4 – Strong & emphatic

The IB function holds complete authority over fees management, which is the top priority. No approvals are required from any individual or department. The IB role is the sole approver, and payment processing is a critical IB activity.
