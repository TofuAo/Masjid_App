# Financial Report Structure (Shareholder / Stakeholder Reports)

This document defines the structure for financial reports to shareholders or stakeholders.

---

## 1. Reporting Period & Overview

- Reporting period (e.g., January–March 2026)
- Brief summary (1 paragraph):
  - Total income
  - Total expenses
  - Net profit or loss
  - Amount available for distribution

---

## 2. Money In (Income)

Shareholders should see exactly how money was earned.

**Include:**
- Total revenue
- Breakdown by source (examples):
  - Sales
  - Services
  - Investments
  - Other income
- Comparison to previous period (optional but helpful)

**Example:**

| Income Source  | Amount   |
|----------------|----------|
| Product Sales  | $50,000  |
| Services       | $20,000  |
| Other Income   | $2,000   |
| **Total Income** | **$72,000** |

---

## 3. Money Out (Expenses)

Show where the money was spent.

**Include:**
- Operating expenses
- Administrative costs
- Taxes
- Loan repayments
- One-time or unusual expenses (clearly labeled)

**Example:**

| Expense Type    | Amount    |
|-----------------|-----------|
| Salaries        | $25,000   |
| Rent & Utilities| $8,000    |
| Marketing       | $5,000    |
| Taxes           | $6,000    |
| **Total Expenses** | **$44,000** |

---

## 4. Net Result (Profit or Loss)

This is the key figure shareholders care about.

- **Net Profit / Loss** = Total Income – Total Expenses
- State clearly whether it's positive or negative

**Example:**
- Total Income: $72,000
- Total Expenses: $44,000
- **Net Profit: $28,000**

---

## 5. Distribution to Shareholders

Explain how profits are shared.

**Include:**
- Total profit available for distribution
- Amount retained by the company
- Amount distributed to shareholders

---

## 6. Shareholder-Specific Payment Details

Each shareholder should see their exact entitlement.

**Include:**
- Number of shares owned
- Percentage ownership
- Dividend or payment per share
- Total amount payable to that shareholder

**Example:**

| Item                 | Value    |
|----------------------|----------|
| Shares Owned         | 1,000    |
| Total Shares         | 10,000   |
| Ownership Percentage | 10%      |
| Dividend per Share   | $2.00    |
| **Total Payment**    | **$2,000** |

---

## 7. Percentages & Ratios (Very Important)

These help shareholders judge performance.

**Common percentages:**
- Profit margin (%)
- Expense ratio (%)
- Growth or decline vs last period (%)
- Return on equity (optional for advanced reports)

---

## 8. Cash Position

Shareholders should know how much cash the company has.

**Include:**
- Opening cash balance
- Closing cash balance
- Cash reserves
- Any cash restrictions

---

## 9. Notes & Transparency

- Explanations for large changes
- Risks or upcoming expenses
- Accounting method used (cash or accrual)
- Confirmation the report is accurate and approved

---

## 10. Sign-off & Contact Information

- Prepared by (name & title)
- Approval signature (director/accountant)
- Date
- Contact details for questions
